How to play

  • 👆Tap anywhere to walk there.
  • ❗Tap a colleague to talk. Each one has three quick questions and a brief fragment.
  • ⚡Faster correct answers score more. Collect all six before time runs out.
  • ☕The coffee cart gives you 30 extra seconds, once.
